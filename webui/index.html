How it works

The model was fitted to 6,093 break-up dates from 64 Finnish lake sites (SYKE, 1900-2026). It uses latitude, longitude, altitude and the year, with a trend that became steeper after 1980. A new lake gets an unknown lake effect, and a new year gets unknown national and regional weather, so the interval shows how much a single spring can vary. Every prediction averages over 600 posterior draws of the model parameters.
